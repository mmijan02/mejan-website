About me

Political questions. Careful research. Public purpose.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of democratic legitimacy, political behavior, public opinion, and the ways policies affect people’s lives.

Background

My path into research

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Political Science and International Studies at the University of Idaho, with an expected graduation year of 2027. My research interests include electoral politics, comparative politics, political psychology, public policy, foreign policy, human rights, refugee policy, and public health risk perception.

As a research assistant, I work with faculty on literature reviews, dataset management, quantitative and qualitative research, conference papers, and manuscripts. My academic activities also include editorial review and research training in public and environmental health.

Alongside research, I have been involved in student leadership, humanitarian service, and refugee-related policy discussions. These experiences inform the questions I ask about representation, trust, and institutions.
