Research agenda

Questions that drive my work

I use quantitative and qualitative approaches to understand the relationship between citizens, institutions, and public policy.

01 / RESEARCH INTEREST

Democratic legitimacy

Why do people trust or question election outcomes? I study how electoral institutions, partisan loyalties, and perceptions of fairness shape public attitudes.

02 / RESEARCH INTEREST

Public opinion & political behavior

How do political identities, feelings, and experiences influence citizens’ views of democracy and their willingness to participate?

03 / RESEARCH INTEREST

Public policy & risk perception

How do people understand policy risks, particularly in public health, and how can research methods help explain their preferences?
